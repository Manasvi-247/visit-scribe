# PCOS and anaemia
# noise: quiet
P: Doctor, my periods are irregular, kabhi do mahine tak nahi aate. Aur weight bhi badh raha hai.
D: Aapke ultrasound mein PCOS dikh raha hai, and haemoglobin is 9.8, so you are anaemic too.
D: Glycomet 500, one tablet at night after dinner, for three months.
D: Orofer XT, one tablet every morning after breakfast, three months.
P: Doctor, iron tablet ka koi side effect? Constipation toh nahi hoga?
D: Thoda ho sakta hai, toh paani zyada piyo and fruits khao. Iron chai ke saath mat lena.
D: Limcee one tablet, Orofer ke saath hi lena, it helps absorption.
D: TSH aur fasting blood sugar bhi karwa lo.
D: Roz 45 minutes exercise, and sugar aur maida kam karo.
D: Follow up after three months with Hb report.
