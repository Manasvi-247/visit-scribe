D: When the headache starts, take Naxdom five hundred, one tablet, not more than two in a day.
D: To prevent the attacks, start Sibelium ten at bedtime. Sorry, start with Sibelium five, one tablet at bedtime, for two months.
P: Will it make me sleepy?
D: A little in the beginning, that is why it is at night.
D: Keep a headache diary, sleep on time, and don't skip meals.
