# Fungal rash after steroid cream
# noise: quiet
P: Doctor, I have an itchy round rash in the groin and on the waist for a month.
D: Have you applied anything on it?
P: The chemist gave me Betnovate cream. It gets better and then comes back.
D: That is a steroid cream, it makes a fungal infection worse. Stop Betnovate completely.
D: Apply Candid cream morning and night, on the rash and two fingers beyond it, for four weeks.
D: Take Forcan one fifty, one tablet once a week, on Sundays, for four weeks.
P: Doctor, so four tablets in total?
D: Yes, four tablets. And Cetzine, one tablet at night for the itching, for ten days.
D: Wear loose cotton clothes, dry the area well after bathing, and wash your towels separately.
D: Come back after four weeks.
