# Child fever and cough
# noise: fan
P: Doctor, mere bete ko do din se bukhar aur khansi hai. Chaar saal ka hai, solah kilo.
D: Fever kitna jaa raha hai?
P: Raat ko ek sau do tak gaya. Maine aadhi Combiflam de di thi.
D: Bachche ko Combiflam mat dijiye. Fever ke liye Calpol syrup, saadhe saat ml, jab fever sau se upar ho.
P: Doctor sahab, injection toh nahi lagega? Aur kitni der mein dobara de sakte hain?
D: Injection ki zaroorat nahi. Do dose ke beech kam se kam chhe ghante ka gap rakhna.
D: Khansi ke liye Ambrodil S syrup, ek chammach matlab paanch ml, din mein teen baar. Nahi, din mein do baar kar dijiye, subah aur raat.
D: Ye paanch din dena hai.
D: Paani zyada pilaiye, garam soup dete rahiye, thanda kuch mat dena.
D: Agar aaram na mile toh teen din baad dobara dikha dena.
